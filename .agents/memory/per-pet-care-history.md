---
name: Per-pet care history
description: The product rule for associating supply usage with a pet and preserving accurate care dates.
---

Care usage belongs to the selected Pet Details photo, not to a Care or Health item globally. Shared supplies must maintain separate histories and totals for every pet. Logging begins from the selected pet, while the per-pet history is displayed in the shared Care or Health item's details.

**Why:** A single leash, brush, or health product can be used for multiple pets. A global item count would merge their histories and make the recorded care misleading.

**How to apply:** New care actions must begin from an identified pet. On a Care or Health item's details, show every pet's name, Last logged date, and Times logged so shared-item history is easy to review. Keep dated usage logs separate from manual total corrections so a total edit never changes the last actual care date.
